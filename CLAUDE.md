# Rules for Claude Code on LynkOmni
- The user is a beginner. Explain each change simply, one small step at a time, and run the tests after every change.
- Money is integer minor units plus a currency code. Never use floats for money.
- The event log (core/events.py) is the source of truth. Add features as new event types and projections.
- Devices may only send event types listed in CLIENT_EVENTS. Never let a client write server-only events.
- Never block an offline sale. Flag conflicts (for example negative stock) for the owner instead.
- Country specifics live in core/regions.py only. No country names or currencies hard-coded elsewhere.
- Real providers go behind the Provider and FiscalAdapter interfaces. Keep simulators for tests.
- LynkOmni never holds customer funds. Financing and insurance work only through licensed partners with merchant consent.
- Do not claim a tax receipt is valid until the tax authority's workflow is confirmed.
- Anything that moves money needs human security review before release.
- Locale changes presentation only. Never change data, identifiers, event types or logic by language, currency or time zone.
- Every new service or sub-service needs a country flag, a sandbox provider, and tests. Off by default until its partner and licence exist.
- Company-level views read aggregates only and must be role-checked and audit-logged.
- Purchases that need a provider never claim success until the provider confirms. Paid but undelivered means a refund_due event.
- Ads are contextual only, always labeled, capped, and never inside payment, void, cash-count, refund, fiscal or private-chat screens. Off per country until legal sign-off. Devices can never bill; only validated impressions count.
- The contract in contract/ is the authority for merchant-core events. Never edit a published version: add a new schema_version and an upcaster note, then run the validator.
- Money in the contract is integer RWF and quantities are integer thousandths. Never use floats in an event.
- Billing: never bill a product twice. The highest tier wins; LynkSuite replaces its nine services and LynkOps; LynkOps (a tier, not a product) replaces LynkAcc, LynkInv, LynkCRM and LynkStat; LynkPOS Pro and Max include LynkRewards Plus and Pro; add-on families stay outside LynkSuite. Count each paid subscription once.
- Developer apps read merchant data only with the merchant's per-scope consent, and every API call is metered and audited.
- Public identifiers come only from core/ids.py (or the TypeScript, Rust, Kotlin and Swift ports of the same registry). Never invent an identifier format.
- Agents never change credentials or complete recovery in chat. Support sees customer data only through a time-limited consent record.
- LynkVCard is an add-on family, never inside LynkSuite. Cards come only from a licensed issuer; LynkOmni never holds funds or stores a card number. Only a freeze may queue offline, and the UI never shows it as frozen until confirmed. Cards stay off in a country until its issuer is signed.
- The footer copyright text is fixed: never reword or translate it.
- Chat is customer-business and support only. Never claim end-to-end encryption. Never add personal or group chat.
- Production code is TypeScript (services, web), Rust (offline core), Kotlin, Swift, SQL. Python is for AI, data, automation and the reference spec.
- When the Python reference and another language disagree, the shared vectors in contract/vectors decide.
- Colours and fonts come only from design/tokens.v1.json. Run `python3 design/generate.py` after any change; never hand-edit generated files. Never load fonts from a third-party site. Every language, its script, its writing direction and its fonts live only in design/fonts/scripts.v1.json (Latin: Montserrat headings, Inter body; Arabic script: Noto Kufi Arabic and Noto Sans Arabic; Hebrew: Noto Sans Hebrew; Devanagari: Noto Sans Devanagari). design/fonts/build_fonts.py builds the WOFF2 files, fonts.css and ts/src/fonts.generated.ts from it; never edit or swap those by hand. A country-pack language missing from the registry fails the tests. Set lang and dir on the root of every screen with ts/src/fonts.ts rootAttributes(); use logical CSS (inline-start/end) so right-to-left works; never letter-space or uppercase Arabic or Devanagari text; offline apps store the files from fontFilesFor(languages). Claim WCAG 2.2 AA; never claim AAA compliance.
- LynkMesh carries data, never money, takes no fee, and a relayed sale shows "sent, awaiting cloud" until the cloud accepts it. Batches must be sealed with services/sealing.py; plain JSON is refused outside tests.
- There is no LynkShield. Offline risk limits only cap unconfirmed non-cash value; a sale stays pending_confirmation and nothing is guaranteed.
- LynkProof only hands evidence packs to the provider; the $5 fee is charged once, only after the provider reports a win. Never claim zero disputes. LynkAudit is not a tax authority device. LynkSplit executes through a licensed provider and LynkOmni holds no funds.
- Only LOPRF-, LOAUD- and LOSPLT- were added for these improvements, and LODOC- (business documents) and LOREV- (verified reviews) on 2026-11-08; LORPT- stays the safety report, LOFBK- product feedback, LOVER- verification and LOCASE- support cases, never borrowed for anything else. Do not create ids for Shield, Snap, One, Stream, Edge, Usage or Portal, and give Portal no separate domain (it lives in business settings and the account site)..
- Device databases are encrypted (SQLCipher) in production; never open one without a key outside tests. State shown to users uses only: queued, sent, accepted, confirmed, failed.
- The new revenue services have no families of their own. LynkProof and LynkSplit are tier features of LynkMPay, LynkAudit of LynkAcc, extra seats of LynkPOS and LynkChat, LynkEdge bandwidth and metering of LynkDev. Their numbers live only in services/billing.py FEATURES. LynkSuite standard prices are $0/$29/$75/$195 (kept when LynkStat joined on 2026-11-08: about 37% below its nine services bought with the LynkOps tier).
- Invoicing is LynkInv, a standalone service (services/invoicing.py) that is also inside LynkOps and LynkSuite; LynkCheckout keeps pay links and QR, and every invoice is paid through them. Issued invoices never change (credit note instead). An electronic payment counts only when the provider confirms it. Never call an invoice a "tax invoice" unless the country's workflow is confirmed in writing. Public id is LOINV-; the printed number is a separate sequential number per merchant series; unused leased numbers are voided with a reason. Invoice limits live only in billing.FEATURES.
- Logos and icons come only from the brand module (brand/ in Python, ts/src/brand.ts in TypeScript), which reads the vendored, hash-locked @lynkomni/icons release in brand/vendor. Never hard-code the brand violet `#7C4DFF`, a logo file name or a logo image URL, and never fetch a logo at run time. Never hand-edit brand/vendor or brand.lock.json: run the brand-sync workflow. `logo-ink` is for the logo only (text uses text-primary, brand-ink is the accessible violet for text). Merchant logos are PNG, JPEG or WebP only, never SVG. Invoice logo rules live in brand.INVOICE_BRANDING.
- Workflow warnings are bugs. After any workflow change or push, check the Actions run for annotations (for example "Node.js 20 is deprecated") and fix them in the same task by moving to the current major version of the action. Do not call a run clean while it shows a warning.
- Product code asks the brand module for a place, never for a file: `brand.for_surface("web-header", theme, width_px)` (TypeScript `chooseAsset`/`surfaceVariant`). Which of logo, emblem and wordmark each place uses lives only in brand/surfaces.v1.json. Logo is the default, emblem for small or crowded spaces (and beside a written name), wordmark where the name must be read (receipts, email footer, "Powered by", partner lockups). Never put the emblem and wordmark side by side to rebuild the logo, and never draw a product name inside an image. The "Powered by LynkOmni" wordmark follows billing.FEATURES powered_by (required on Free, removable on paid) and never appears on payment, refund, void, cash-count, fiscal or private-chat screens.
- Prices are LOCKED (version 3, 2026-11-08: LynkStat $0/$12/$29/$79 took over LynkDash; LynkOps $0/$20/$49/$129 with four services): every standard USD price in services/billing.py FAMILIES is pinned by tests/test_price_lock.py. Change a price only when the user says so, and in the same task update the pin, finance/revenue_model.py (re-run it), the blueprint (pricing tables, LynkSuite table, revenue model parts E, F and G), the project status doc and this file. Never quote revenue without saying the adoption mix is an assumption.
- Icons (product marks, status icons, core UI icons) come only from the brand module: `brand.icon(id)` and `brand.status_icon(state)` (TypeScript later). Status icons exist only for queued, sent, accepted, confirmed, failed; never invent another state or icon. Icons are one colour (currentColor), so they take the text colour; always show the state word or an accessible name beside a status icon, never colour alone. Product marks never have a product name drawn inside; the name is text beside them. Never hand-edit brand/vendor.
- Visual assets (icons, illustrations, empty states, charts, diagrams, figures, share and social images, store graphics) are created by Claude whenever development needs them; never leave a placeholder or a "TODO image". The user supplies only the logos. Each asset must fit its exact purpose and the LynkOmni system: colours and fonts only from design/tokens.v1.json, logos only through the brand module, icons added to lynkomni-icons (with its gates) and vendored, other graphics made as original, bundled, offline-capable files (SVG first). Never use stock, scraped or third-party art, and never show photos of real people. Charts and figures use real data from the code or tests, never invented numbers, and say when a figure is an assumption. Every asset needs a text alternative, 3:1 contrast, no meaning by colour alone, and a size budget. Anything not yet reviewed by a human designer is labelled a draft.
- Visuals come only from the visuals module (visuals/ in Python, ts/src/visuals.ts in TypeScript): `visuals.get(id, theme, mode)`; never build a path or draw a picture inline. To get a new picture, add a need to visuals/needs.v1.json (id, kind, scene or figure, accurate alt text, purpose, used_in, size budget) and run `python3 visuals/generate.py`; never edit visuals/generated by hand. Every product family needs a `product-<name>` illustration, so a new family fails the tests until it has one. `python3 visuals/check.py` must pass: it blocks missing, orphaned, off-token, wordy, oversized or stale pictures, placeholder or "TODO image" text, stock-image services, web images, and calls for ids that do not exist. Illustrations contain no words, faces or people; chart and diagram text comes from code (billing, revenue model, state tokens) and assumption figures say so. Lite mode hides decorative images (`visuals.decorative`), sunlight mode uses the contrast set. Everything is a draft until a human designer signs off. App-store screenshots of real screens and any photograph are not made here: screenshots are captured from the real app later, and photos are never used.
- LynkVCard faces (visuals/cards.py) follow the approved spec: Free = EDGE (slate, one dot), Plus = HUB (silver, hub with three lines), Pro = CORE (jet black, glowing core and spend ring), Max = OMNI (obsidian sphere, no number dots on the front). Colours are the vcard-*-bg and on-vcard-* tokens. A face never shows a card number, card holder name, bank name or payment-network mark (the licensed issuer adds those and prints the last four); its only words are the fixed tier line in cards.TIER_TEXT (the one exception to "never draw a name inside an image"); the emblem comes from brand surface `vcard-face` (one colour). Tier prices and limits shown anywhere are read from services/billing.py and labelled placeholders. The app animation (dot, three lines, hub, fill, core, sphere) is built later, must stop under reduced motion and in lite mode, and falls back to the still faces.
- Provider placeholders (providers/registry.v1.json, gate `python3 providers/check.py`, CI job `providers`): work that needs a provider is built now as a working sandbox adapter behind its interface, and stays OFF for real users until the item is `contract_signed` or `live` for that country (`providers.ready(service, country)`; `core.regions.enabled` checks it, so a flag alone never switches a service on). Phase 1 services (pos, accountant, invoicing, crm, inventory, dashboard, intelligence, chat) are never gated by a provider. Every PLACEHOLDER or SANDBOX note in the code must belong to a ledger entry. Never show users fake provider text or marks (no "[Bank]", no made-up network logos); when a service is off, show the `when_off` state. Non-provider work that must happen before go-live (hardware signing, SQLCipher, security review, designer review, fonts, trademark checks) lives under `later` in the same file; update the ledger in the same change whenever a provider or placeholder is added, replaced or removed, and give every real adapter an importable `real_adapter` before raising a status.
- Default way of working (user rule): build everything that can be built now, as development goes. Work that needs a provider gets a working sandbox placeholder (see the provider ledger) and is integrated when the provider is assigned. Only work that truly cannot be done now and cannot use a placeholder is left out, and every such item goes in the `later` list of providers/registry.v1.json at once, so it is done when its time comes. Any step that needs the user (a new repo, an account, an organisation setting, a signature, a file only they have) is listed in the `user_actions` list of the same file and said to the user in the same reply; never leave it unmentioned.
- Every service or section with a user interface gets a real, working interface emulator, not only back-end code. "Real" means it looks and behaves like the real-life device it runs on: for example LynkPOS as a physical point-of-sale terminal (screen, keypad, card tap area, receipt printer that prints, scanner, cash drawer), the virtual card as a real card. The screens inside run on the sandbox adapters. Device bodies are original LynkOmni designs, never a copy of a real maker's product, logo or network mark. Emulators live in web/emulators/<id>/ (shell.html, style.css, app.ts using the real rules in ts/src), are built into one offline file per emulator with `node web/build.mjs` (never edit web/emulators/dist by hand), and must pass tests/test_emulators.py: tokens only, no web loads, no provider or device-maker names, a "Draft design" label. Hardware colours are the device-* tokens; the casing mark is brand surface `device-body`. A virtual card screen never receives the card number: details are shown only after step-up authentication, inside the licensed issuer's secure display, hidden again after a short time, and never offline.
- Merchant logos pass services/merchant_logo.validate (PNG, JPEG, WebP by file bytes, size and shape limits, no markup, no animation) every time they are stored and again every time they are shown; they appear only on tiers whose invoice_branding allows it.
- Working standards (owner approved 2026-11-06, docs/lynkomni-standards.md): every change is made on a branch `lynkomni-<topic>`, goes through a pull request with the finish checklist, and is merged into main only when every check is green with no warnings; each finished piece gets a version (`0.MINOR.PATCH`, Git tag `v...`) and a plain line in docs/lynkomni-changelog.md; every owner decision gets a dated line in docs/lynkomni-decisions.md (never deleted, a change gets a new line). Dependency updates (Dependabot drafts) are merged without asking once their checks are green and nothing else is pending (owner, 2026-11-08), and each batch gets a change-log line.
- Global availability (owner approved 2026-11-06): LynkOmni is one global product, designed to global standards with 100% global appeal; nothing is designed for one country. Every service is shown in every country (`providers.availability(service, country)`). Services that need no licensed partner work everywhere, including countries without a pack. Regulated services (payments, cards, lending, insurance, crypto, exchange, tax receipts) show "Coming to your country soon." with Notify me (services/waitlist.py: consent required, contact only as a PII vault reference, told once) until their licensed partner is signed there, then switch on by themselves. Never write "not available" to users. Prefer partners that cover many countries at once.
- Translation (owner decisions 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-07): no native-speaker review. Screen text lives in web/emulators/<id>/strings.en.json and is translated by Claude into every language of design/fonts/scripts.v1.json; write the translations as {key: text} files and run tools/translation/claude_packs.py, which checks them and writes web/emulators/<id>/i18n/<lang>.json (never edit a pack by hand, never leave a key in English). After changing English screen text, translate the changed keys for every language in the same change (tests/test_translation_packs.py fails on missing, stale, English or broken packs). Text people type (chat, product names, notes) uses the machine engine through services/translation.py: Google MADLAD-400-3B-MT (Apache 2.0) as CTranslate2 int8 (MadladTranslator, folder LYNK_MT_DIR); never use Meta NLLB-200 or any model whose licence forbids commercial use. The model is downloaded and converted only by tools/translation/install_engine.py (licence checked, revision pinned), never by app code; the "translation" workflow installs it and runs tools/translation/check_engine.py. Every translation passes services/translation.run: brand, product and provider names (GLOSSARY), {placeholders} and numbers come back unchanged, no repetition, no added words or sentences, and for the engine a round trip back to English; otherwise the original text is kept. Translations must never change a screen's size or break its layout: screens use logical CSS and wrapping, and tests/test_layout_i18n.py (CI job layout) checks every emulator screen in every language, a long-text test and a right-to-left test at desktop and phone size. SandboxTranslator (pseudo-translations) is for tests and machines without the engine.
- LynkAcc (accounting; took over LynkLedger), LynkInv (invoicing), LynkCRM (customers) and LynkStat (analytics; took over LynkDash, app.lynkomni.com/analytics) are standalone services, each Free, Plus, Pro, Max and Enterprise: one product for every size, never separate small and big editions. LynkOps is only the tier that bundles the four. They are paths in the Business app, never subdomains: app.lynkomni.com/accounting, /invoicing and /crm, the same words as their product pages. Address rules (owner approved 2026-11-07): the service is one singular word; lists inside it are plural nouns; one record opens by its public id from core/ids.py; creating is /new; an issued invoice has no edit address (credit note instead); filters are ?view=; addresses are lowercase English joined by hyphens and the same in every language; an old address redirects forever (/ledger to /accounting).
- LynkPOS agent point mode and its agent, collection and partner services are online only and available at every tier. The cash belongs to the agent and the electronic float sits in the agent's own account at the licensed partner; the partner checks identity, sets limits, confirms each transaction and pays the agent's commission; LynkOmni bills the partner for its share and never pools, holds or settles money. Cash changes hands only after the partner confirms.
- Real-device emulators follow the product (owner rule 2026-11-07): whenever a change alters a service's rules, screens, services, prices or limits, update that service's emulators in the same change, so every emulator always shows the product as it is now. New emulators are built in the build section where their service is built (master blueprint section 42 J lists them per section), never ahead of the build order.
- LynkPOS services (agreed 2026-11-07, master blueprint section 42 I): 31 services in 6 groups, agent point mode, suggestions S1 to S12. Agent, collection and partner-finance requests are never queued offline; the device writes AgentRequestCreated and the cloud alone writes the partner's answer (AgentRequestConfirmed, AgentRequestFailed, CommissionRecorded and the other partner events); never offer "hand over cash" before the partner confirms.
- One catalog, one address map, one contacts map (owner approved 2026-11-07): catalog/products.v1.json (every billing family with its page, app path, menu group, illustration and footer place), catalog/addresses.v1.json (every host, website path and permanent redirect; legal pages live at /legal/(page)) and catalog/contacts.v1.json (every email address, the page that shows it, where it lands and how it is answered). Change them first and the footer, menus, launcher, pages and emails follow; tests/test_catalog.py fails when anything disagrees. Never put an email address or a website path in code that is not in these files.
- Support levels come from billing FEATURES `support` (help, priority, higher, dedicated by tier for every family); the highest across an account's plans wins and sets its queue priority. Never hard-code a list of families for support.
- The blueprint quotes counts, prices and versions from `python3 tools/blueprint_numbers.py`, never typed by hand. The "$500 billion" standard is a quality bar (one source of truth, no contradictions, everything connected, every message routed, every address clean, global by design, safe and trusted, honest numbers), re-checked at the end of every build section; it is not a revenue target.
- An agent's commission is paid by the licensed partner, never by LynkOmni; LynkOmni bills the partner for its own share and posts commission to LynkAcc only from the cloud's CommissionRecorded event. LynkAcc, LynkInv and LynkCRM work fully in any signed-in browser; selling, cash, shifts and agent services run only on an enrolled device. Business app tabs follow the business type (shop, no till, agent point).
- Second review (owner approved 2026-11-08, master blueprint section 44): every username (shop, business, person, developer app, partner, agent point) uses the exact Instagram format, 3 to 30 characters: a to z, 0 to 9, full stops and underscores, lowercase, no full stop at the start or end and never two in a row; it must be free before it is taken, belongs to its owner forever and is never reissued to anyone else; reserved words and web-address-like endings are refused; names stay free text. Merchant and customer data is never sold: supplier sell-through analytics shows suppliers only their own products, from merchants who choose to share. Partner logos appear only with the partner's written brand licence through the Partner Hub (otherwise the name as text). Every outside organisation is added, measured and removed only through the Partner Hub (staff.lynkomni.com/partner-hub, partner door partners.lynkomni.com), per country, behind interfaces with sandbox connectors; unplugging finishes every request already sent, revokes keys and removes the partner's assets everywhere while legal records keep the name as text. LynkChat translates between two languages with a "Translated from" note and a switch back, nothing shown when both use the same language, on LynkOmni's own servers only. The 14 new services (N1 to N14) live inside existing families; their limits are in billing FEATURES and SERVICE_FEES, and each is built in the build section listed in blueprint 44 L. Revenue, expenses and net profit come from finance/revenue_model.py and finance/expense_model.py (assumptions labelled).
- Automation and LynkAssist (owner approved 2026-11-08, master blueprint section 45): every automation is registered in catalog/automations.v1.json (trigger, action, level, LynkAssist role, what it touches, consent basis, build section) and runs on LynkFlow from events or schedules; tests/test_automations.py enforces the rules. Levels are inform, draft (a person confirms), auto_undo (owner's limits, undoable) and auto (housekeeping and messages the person already chose). Credentials, recovery, tax filing, payroll, finance requests, exchange, personal data and consent are only ever inform or draft; money moves only on a person's confirmation or the customer's own capped standing instruction held by the licensed provider, told every time. Customer messages need a transactional basis or opt-in, with quiet hours and the reader's language. LynkAssist answers, guides, drafts, explains, summarises and watches; it never moves money, changes credentials, completes recovery, files with an authority, decides credit or acts beyond its level, and its suggestions are labelled. Every run is an event, idempotent and loop-guarded, with "Why did this happen?", history at app.lynkomni.com/business/automations and an off switch. Never add an automation outside the register.
- Anything left out is adjusted automatically during the build (owner rule 2026-11-08): when a build section meets a line, number, address, rule, emulator, translation or automation that the blueprint or the code missed or still states the old way, correct it in that section's own change by the latest agreed decision, add a dated line to docs/lynkomni-decisions.md and docs/lynkomni-changelog.md, and tell the owner in the section's review. Never lock a build section while such a leftover remains, and never park it for a separate review round.
