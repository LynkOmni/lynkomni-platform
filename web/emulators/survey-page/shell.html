LynkSurvey on your phone Draft design

Interactive emulator of the page a person opens from a link, a QR code, LynkChat or an email: no app and no account. It says who is asking, why and how long it takes, asks for agreement before a sensitive question, sends a code when the survey allows one answer per phone, asks one question a page and ends with thank you and Don't ask me again. The box beside it shows what the organisation sees. Sample survey, sandbox partners. Not reviewed by a designer yet.
