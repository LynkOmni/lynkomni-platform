Customer's tracking page Draft design

Address:

Interactive emulator of the page a customer opens from a tracking link, with no account (shop.lynkomni.com/track/...): each step of the order and its delivery, the promised time, live progress as a share of the trip (never a position), a missed promise, and a choice to hear each step by text message or chat. Sample data, sandbox partners, test money only. Not reviewed by a designer yet.
