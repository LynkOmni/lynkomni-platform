LynkPOS handheld terminal Draft design

Interactive emulator. Real LynkPOS sale rules, sandbox payment providers (test money only), sample products. Not reviewed by a designer yet.

/*@BODYMARK@*/

Try it

Unlock with PIN 1234. Tap products, then Basket, Charge and a payment method. Your keyboard works too: digits, Enter, Backspace, Esc.

The yellow key on the left side scans a barcode. For a card payment, click the four lights above the screen (tap) or the slot at the bottom (insert).

Conditions

Event log (what the terminal sends to the cloud)
