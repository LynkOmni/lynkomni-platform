LynkOmni merchant app on a phone Draft design

Interactive emulator. Real LynkOmni rules for selling, stock and the day's summary, sandbox providers (test money only), sample products and sample customer chats. Not reviewed by a designer yet.

Try it

Sign in with any phone number and the sample code 123456. Then sell, change stock, answer a customer and open Business for the day's summary.

Conditions

Weak counts as offline: the phone saves everything and sends when it can. None: nothing is sent until the connection is back.
