Try it

Everything here is the shared shell: the header with the side you are on (your workspace or your Storefront), the navigation that becomes tabs on a phone, the app launcher (grid), the account menu with Change view and Why this view?, language and region (globe), "find or do anything" (search), the help panel, notifications, the connection chip and every screen state. On a touch view, swipe from the edge to open the menu, swipe a sheet down to close it and press and hold a tab for its help; with a keyboard, arrows move inside a group and "/" opens search.
