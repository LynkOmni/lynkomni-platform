LynkVCard as a real card Draft design

Interactive emulator of the LynkVCard: the four tier faces as a real card that flips to its back, freeze (shown as frozen only when the issuer confirms) and card details shown only after a fresh check inside the licensed issuer's secure display, hidden again after a short time and never offline. No card number ever reaches this screen. Sample data, sandbox partners, test money only. Not reviewed by a designer yet.
