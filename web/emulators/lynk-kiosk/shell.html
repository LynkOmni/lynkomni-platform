LynkKiosk Draft design

Interactive emulator of LynkKiosk, the self-service kiosk: check in or out with LynkAttend, take a ticket with LynkQueue and give an answer with LynkSurvey. It works by touch or by buttons only, with or without internet. Real LynkOmni rules, sample people, sandbox partners. Not reviewed by a designer yet.
